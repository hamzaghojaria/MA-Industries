## Corrections and interpretation

- Zipper bags: three sizes at 60 micrometres, with the dimensions printed in the quotation.
- Gloves: 26 micrometres; adult 200/100/50 counts and kids 30 count.
- Medium roll bags: 27 x 37 cm, replacing the earlier 25 x 35 cm.
- Medium cutting board: 0.9 x 20.7 x 33.5 cm.
- Rice paddles: five variants, including the standard spatula; dimensions follow the quotation.
- Added 20-piece toothbrush packs, eight glass-container capacities and all nine cleaning options.
- Matched-product weights and composition percentages from older website data were removed where the new quotation does not support them.
- Dimensions keep the supplier’s printed order; no length/width/height orientation is inferred.
- Office paper notation “75g” is interpreted as 75 GSM and identified as supplier notation.
- Straw labels Large (21 cm) and Medium (25 cm) are retained exactly; “6ø” has no stated unit. The unit and labels are flagged for confirmation.
- Jumbo tissue: 2,740 mm web width, four layers, quoted per kg. This is not a claim that a complete reel weighs 1 kg.
- Baby seven-piece set dimensions are not provided and remain unspecified.
- The column labelled EAN contains tariff-style codes, not verified EAN barcodes. It is not published as barcode data.
