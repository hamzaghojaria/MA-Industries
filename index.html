A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Before you enquire.

A few questions tend to come up before an order. Here are the details that help you choose a range and prepare your brief.

Are all sugarcane products made from the same material?+

Check the specific item. Paper and bagasse pulp appear in the paper range, and some kitchenware and films use bio-based plastic formulations. A glass container has a glass body and separate lid components. Ask us for the composition if it matters to your use.

What are the sugarcane and recycled ranges?+

Look in Sugarcane & Recycled for kitchen essentials, food-storage formats, paper and household tools. If you need a particular material or care document, tell us which item you are considering.

Which cleaning products should I choose?+

Use Home & Surface Care for floors, kitchens and glass, Hand & Washroom Hygiene for handwash and toilet cleaners, and Commercial & Automotive Care for degreasers, air care and vehicle washing. For regular replenishment, include your preferred pack size.

Not sure which product to choose? +

Choose Help me choose in the enquiry form. Tell us what the product needs to do, what material you prefer if any, and roughly how much you need.

What should I include in my enquiry? +

Give us the product name or intended use, an estimated quantity and the size or finish that matters. Include paper weight (GSM), delivery location and your deadline if you know them.

Can I ask about samples or availability? +

Yes. Name the product and any size or finish you want to see so we can check sample options and current availability.
